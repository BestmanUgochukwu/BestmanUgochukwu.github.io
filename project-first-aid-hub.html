First Aid Hub — BYU Pathway Student Support App

A student-first web application built to simplify access to academic resources, spiritual support, and daily tools for BYU Pathway students worldwide.

The Purpose

Why I Built This

The Question I Asked Myself

"Why create this app when BYU–Pathway Worldwide and Ensign College already have an app?"

The goal of this app is not to replace the existing platforms from BYU–Pathway or Ensign College. Those platforms already serve students in many wonderful ways. My purpose was to build something that focuses specifically on simplicity, accessibility, and the everyday experience of students — especially those who may struggle with internet access, navigation, or finding important information quickly.

Many students — particularly international students — find it difficult to move through multiple platforms just to accomplish simple tasks like checking deadlines, finding resources, or staying spiritually and academically motivated. This app brings key resources together in a way that feels easy and supportive.

This project is fundamentally about service. It is about creating something that helps students access what they need quickly, feel encouraged in their studies, and stay connected to the purpose of education.

"Let all things be done unto edifying." — 1 Corinthians 14:26 The goal of this project: to edify and uplift students by making their educational journey easier.
"By small and simple things are great things brought to pass." — Alma 37:6 This app may be small, but if it helps even one student stay organized, it has fulfilled its purpose.

Features

What the App Does

📚

Quick Resource Access

Direct links to academic resources, course portals, and student tools — no navigating multiple platforms to find what you need.

📅

Deadline Awareness

Helps students stay aware of important dates and academic deadlines without digging through multiple systems.

🌍

International Student Friendly

Designed with low-bandwidth environments in mind. Simple layout that works even on slower connections common in developing countries.

✝️

Spiritual Encouragement

Incorporates motivational and spiritual content aligned with the values of BYU Pathway students — keeping purpose at the center of education.

🧭

Simple Navigation

Clean, intuitive interface that any student can use regardless of technical background. No learning curve — just open and go.

📱

Mobile First

Fully responsive and works on any device. Many BYU Pathway students worldwide access the internet primarily through mobile phones.

How It Was Built

The Build Process

1

Problem Discovery — Talking to Real Students

Started by identifying the real pain points of BYU Pathway students — particularly international students who struggle with slow connections, complex navigation, and finding resources across multiple platforms. The problem was defined before a single line of code was written.

2

Design — Simplicity as a Core Requirement

Designed the layout with a mobile-first, low-bandwidth philosophy. Every UI decision was filtered through one question: "Can a first-generation college student in a developing country use this easily on a 3G connection?" Anything that failed that test was removed.

3

Build — HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no heavy frameworks, no large bundle sizes. This was a deliberate technical choice: frameworks add complexity and download size. For an app targeting students with limited connectivity, lightweight is not optional, it is ethical.

4

Content Curation — What Matters to Students

Curated the resource links, academic tools, and spiritual content by asking: "What do students look for most often?" Academic deadlines, course access, tutoring resources, and motivational content topped the list. Each section was built around a real student need.

5

Testing — Real Students, Real Feedback

Tested the app with actual BYU Pathway students and gathered feedback on navigation clarity, mobile usability, and whether it actually solved the problems it set out to solve. Iterated based on what students said mattered, not what seemed technically impressive.

6

Deployment — Fast Loading, Globally Accessible

Deployed as a static web application for maximum speed and reliability. Static sites load faster, cost less to host, and work better on slow connections than dynamic server-rendered apps. The deployment is the technical expression of the design philosophy: fast, simple, accessible.

Reflection

Service as Engineering

The Best Projects Solve Real Problems for Real People

I did not build this app to impress a panel or pad a portfolio. I built it because I had watched fellow students struggle. The motivation was Mosiah 2:17 — "when ye are in the service of your fellow beings ye are only in the service of your God." That is not a nice sentiment; it is a design requirement.

Technical Simplicity Is a Form of Respect

Choosing plain HTML and CSS over a JavaScript framework was not laziness — it was respect for the students who would use this app on slow connections in developing countries. Every kilobyte you remove from a page load is a small act of service to someone with a 3G connection.

Small Things Compound

Alma 37:6 says "by small and simple things are great things brought to pass." This app may be small. But if it helps even one student stay organized, feel supported, or continue their education without frustration, then it has fulfilled its entire purpose.
