🔧 Week 4 Project Refinement

After reflection, I identified three critical limitations in the original project and refined it accordingly:

Challenge 1 — Single-Metric Bias
RBIs are context-dependent — a player on a weak team has fewer RBI opportunities regardless of skill. Fixed by adding a composite score combining RBI (40%), home runs (30%), and batting average (30%), producing two parallel rankings: volume AND quality.
Challenge 2 — No Era Normalization
The database spans multiple decades. Raw RBI counts from the 1970s cannot be fairly compared to modern players. The refined query now surfaces era context so decision-makers can apply appropriate judgment.
Ethical Issue — Algorithmic Bias
Volume-based metrics systematically disadvantage schools from smaller conferences or underrepresented regions. Added an ethical disclaimer to the report warning against using single-metric rankings as the sole basis for scouting decisions — protecting players from algorithmic unfairness.
✦ Spiritual Reflection: Data without wisdom is dangerous. Just as we should not judge a person by a single metric, we should not rank institutions by a single statistic. Wisdom means asking not just what the data shows — but what it hides, and who it harms.
