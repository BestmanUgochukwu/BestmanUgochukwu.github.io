Foundation

1. Faith & Guidance

"If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God…" — James 1:5

Every complex cloud architecture or DevOps decision I face begins with seeking divine wisdom. At The CloudAdvisory and in church technology coordination, prayer is my first resource, not my last resort.

2. Service & Leadership

"When ye are in the service of your fellow beings…" — Mosiah 2:17

I view IT as a ministry. Managing 50+ laptops for YSA and BYU Pathway students, building the First Aid Hub for student support, and mentoring peers weekly — each is an act of divine service disguised as tech work.

3. Discipline & Consistency

"Men should be anxiously engaged in a good cause…" — D&C 58:27–28

The same discipline that earned me Champion of the Week twice — in Week 6 and Week 13 — drives my spiritual life. Five CompTIA certifications and a 3.91 CGPA prove that small, consistent efforts compound into great results.

Reflection

Weekly Journal

This week's deeper engagement helped me see clearer connections between my faith and IT journey. Completing the CloudAdvisory internship in May 2026 — and being recognized as Champion of the Week in both Week 6 and Week 13 — was not just a professional milestone. It was a confirmation that being "anxiously engaged in a good cause" (D&C 58:27) produces fruit. I did not earn those awards by doing the minimum; I earned them by bringing my whole self to every assignment.

Building the First Aid Hub for BYU Pathway students reminded me that the best projects are the ones born from genuine care for people. Mosiah 2:17 was not a scripture I quoted — it was a design requirement. Every feature of that tool was built with a struggling student in mind. That is the kind of engineer I want to be: technically excellent and genuinely servant-hearted. As Elder Holland wrote, I will not cast away my confidence — even when a deployment fails at 2 AM.
